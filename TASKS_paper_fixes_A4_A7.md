## Task A4 — tutorial 06 on the neo-Hookean energy

### What the paper now says (target)

Fig. 5 and Sec. "Inverse design of a shape-changing elastomer sheet" describe the sheet with the neo-Hookean
in-plane energy, Eq. (9),

    E_nH = h ∫ dA [ G_s/2 (tr C / sqrt(det C) − 2) + G_b/2 (sqrt(det C) − 1)^2 ],   C = g_r^{-1} g,

plus the bending energy Eq. (10), E_B = G_f ∫ dA tr((b − b_r)^2), where "G_f ∝ h^3 is the flexural modulus",
and the caption parameters are **G_s = G_b = G_f = 1, h = 0.1, λ = 1.1** (teacher/student, Fig. 5b) and
**λ = 1.5** (Suzanne, Fig. 5c). G_s is the *shear* modulus (the `tr C/√det C` term) and G_b the *bulk* modulus.

In the library, `elastic.get_neo_hookean_energy(vertices, (hemesh, metric_ref, mod_bulk, mod_shear))`
computes Σ_f A0_f · W(C_f) exactly with that density (note the argument order: bulk first), and
`elastic.get_svk_bending_energy(vertices, (hemesh, metric_ref, b_ref, alpha, beta))` computes
Σ_f A0_f [α tr(S)^2 + 2β tr(S^2)] with S = g_r^{-1}(b − b_r). With **α = 0, β = G_f/2** this is exactly
G_f Σ_f A0_f tr(S^2), i.e. Eq. (10) with the metric used to raise indices.

### What the notebook currently does

- Cells 1–2 (markdown): describe the St.-Venant–Kirchhoff in-plane energy with Lamé coefficients α, β and the
  SVK bending energy, "Koiter" naming, h/4 and h^3/12 prefactors.
- Cell 19 (markdown): "sum of the St.-Venant-Kirchhoff in-plane and bending energies … We minimize with BFGS"
  (the code uses `optimistix.NonlinearCG`).
- Cell 20: `get_shell_energy = h/4 · get_st_venant_kirchhoff_energy(…, 1, 1) + h^3/12 · get_svk_bending_energy(…, 1, 1)`,
  used by `relax_shell` for the teacher/student and ellipsoid examples and by the bimetallic-strip sanity check (cell 22).
- Cell 48 (markdown, "Choice of the in-plane energy"): justifies switching to neo-Hookean *only* for Suzanne.
- Cell 49/50: a second energy `get_shell_energy_nh` (neo-Hookean in-plane + SVK bending, same prefactors) for Suzanne.
